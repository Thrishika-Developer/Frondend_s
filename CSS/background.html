DOG

Dogs are domesticated, highly social mammals known as "man's best friend". They vary greatly in size (from 2 kg Chihuahuas to 90 kg Mastiffs), but typically feature four legs, a tail, a wet nose, and a keen sense of smell. They are celebrated for their loyalty, affection, and intelligence.A dog is a faithful and intelligent domestic animal. It is known as "man's best friend" because of its loyalty and affection. Dogs help humans by guarding homes, assisting in rescue operations, and providing companionship. They need proper food, exercise, grooming, and regular veterinary care to stay healthy.

  • French Bulldog: Small, playful, quiet, and great for apartments.
  • Labrador Retriever: Friendly, active, loving, and good with kids.
  • Golden Retriever: Smart, kind, loyal, and very eager to please.
  • German Shepherd: Brave, smart, very loyal, and easy to train.
  • Dachshund: Small, lively, smart, and full of courage.
  • Poodle: Highly smart, active, and comes in three sizes.
  • Beagle: Friendly, happy, and has a great sense of smell.
  • Rottweiler: Strong, calm, and a very good protector.
  • Pointer (German Shorthaired): High energy, smart, and loves to run.
  • Pembroke Welsh Corgi: Small, smart, friendly, and very active.
